Tuning

The tuner doesn't react, or only to loud notes

  • Hold the phone close to the instrument, with the microphone (usually at the bottom of the phone) pointing towards it.
  • Play one string at a time and let it ring.
  • Background noise, such as talking, a TV or a fan, can drown out a quiet string.
  • Check that the app is allowed to use the microphone (see below).

The note jumps to a different octave or string

Pluck firmly and let the note ring rather than muting it quickly. The very start of a pluck and the fading end are the hardest parts to read, so the tuner holds the last steady reading for a moment. For very low notes, like a bass's low B or E, pluck a little harder or hold the phone closer to the body of the instrument.

What do the numbers on the needle mean?

They're cents: 100 cents is one semitone (one fret). Negative means flat (too low), positive means sharp (too high). Within about ±3 cents sounds in tune to most people.

What is A4 or the reference pitch?

It's the pitch every other note is measured from. Almost all music uses A4 = 440 Hz, which is the default. Change it only if you're playing along with a piano, recording or band tuned differently. When it isn't 440 Hz, the app highlights it so you don't forget.

Can I tune other instruments?

Yes. Chromatic mode shows the nearest note to whatever you play, for any instrument.

Metronome

The metronome sounds late through Bluetooth headphones or speakers

Bluetooth audio adds a delay, often a tenth of a second or more, and no app can remove it. For practice, use the phone's speaker or wired headphones.

Does it keep playing with the screen off?

Yes. On Android you'll see it in your notifications, with a button to stop it.

Microphone access

The tuner needs the microphone to hear your instrument. If you said no when the app asked:

  • Android: Settings › Apps › Offline Tuner & Metronome › Permissions › Microphone › Allow only while using the app.
  • iPhone: Settings › Offline Tuner & Metronome › Microphone: on.

Nothing the microphone hears is recorded or leaves your phone. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, everything works in flight mode. The app never goes online.
