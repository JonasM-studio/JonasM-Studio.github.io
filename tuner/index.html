Offline Tuner & Metronome

A tuner and metronome for guitar, bass and ukulele. No ads, no account, and no internet connection, ever.

  • Works onAndroid 10+ first, iPhone later
  • InternetNever used
  • AdsNone

What it does

  • Tuner for guitar, bass and ukulele, with alternate tunings such as Drop D, DADGAD and Open G, a chromatic mode for any instrument, and an adjustable reference pitch (A4).
  • Metronome that keeps steady time and keeps playing when the screen is off.
  • Works in flight mode. Everything happens on your phone. On Android the app doesn't even have the internet permission.

Availability

The Android version is in testing. It will be on Google Play first, then the App Store. Questions or feedback: jonasm.studio@gmail.com.
