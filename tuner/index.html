Evenpitch

A tuner and metronome for guitar, bass and ukulele. No ads or account, and no internet connection required.

  • Works onAndroid and iPhone
  • InternetNot required
  • AdsNone

What it does

  • Tuner for guitar, bass and ukulele, with alternate tunings such as Drop D, DADGAD and Open G, a chromatic mode for any instrument, and an adjustable reference pitch (A4).
  • Metronome with steady timing that continues when the screen is off.
  • Works offline, including in flight mode. Sound is processed on your device, and the Android version does not request internet access.

Availability

Evenpitch is currently in testing ahead of release on Google Play and the App Store. For questions or feedback, email yujona.studio@gmail.com.
