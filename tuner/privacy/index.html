Privacy Policy

Offline Tuner & Metronome for iPhone and Android · Last updated 2 November 2026

Offline Tuner & Metronome doesn't collect any data, and it never goes online.

It listens to your instrument to tune it, works out the pitch on your phone, and keeps nothing it hears.

What the app uses, and why

Microphone

Used only to hear the note you're playing, so the tuner can show its pitch. The sound is analysed in your phone's memory a fraction of a second at a time and then discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Only while the tuner screen is open. Your phone shows its microphone indicator whenever the microphone is in use.

Media controls (Android)

When the metronome keeps playing with the screen off, Android shows it as a media notification so you can see and stop it, like a music player.

Your settings

Your reference pitch (A4), instrument, tuning and tempo are stored on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

It really is offline

On Android, the app doesn't have the internet permission at all, so the system won't let it connect to anything. You can check this on the app's Google Play page under "About this app" › "App permissions". On iPhone, the app makes no network requests, which you can check with App Privacy Report in Settings › Privacy & Security.

What the app never does

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No recording or uploading of sound.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code from other companies.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We don't have any to sell.

Purchases and crash reports

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, not through the app itself. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The stores give us sales reports, such as how many copies were sold in which country. The app checks your purchase on your phone.

Crash reports: if you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash reports that show where in the app's code a crash happened. They contain no sound. You can turn this off in your phone's privacy settings.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to reply, and delete it if you ask. Our email is hosted by Gmail (Google).

Children

Musicians of all ages use tuners. The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children, and has no ads, outside links to buy things, or social features.

Your rights

There's no app data held by us to ask about. For anything you've emailed us, you can ask to see it or have it deleted. This applies wherever you live, including the EU and UK.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Every past version is kept in the public change history.
