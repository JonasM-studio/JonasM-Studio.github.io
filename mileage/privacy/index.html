Privacy Policy

Mileage Logbook for iPhone and Android · Last updated 2 November 2026

Mileage Logbook doesn't collect any data. Your drives, places and reports stay on your phone, and we never see them.

There's no account and no server. The app needs your location to record drives. The only time it goes online is to ask your phone's built-in map service for street addresses.

What the app uses, and why

Everything below is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.

Location, including in the background

Used to detect when you start and stop driving, and to measure the route and distance of each drive. Background access ("Allow all the time" on Android, "Always" on iPhone) is needed because drives start while the app is closed.

Recorded while you're driving and briefly around the place you parked. Kept on your phone until you delete the trip or the app.

Motion and physical activity

Your phone tells the app when it thinks you're in a vehicle, walking or still. That's how the app knows when to start and stop recording without draining your battery.

The app keeps a log of these changes on your phone so it can point out drives it may have missed.

Street addresses

To show "123 Example St" instead of coordinates, the app asks the address service built into your phone: Apple's on iPhone, and on Android the one your phone provides, usually Google's. That service receives the coordinates of each trip's start and end, under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

After each trip, and when you edit a trip's start or end. Nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

Used to tell you when a drive was recorded, when something has stopped the app from recording, and when it's time to back up.

Things you type in

Vehicles, odometer readings, trip purposes, notes and rates. Stored on your phone only.

Backups, exports and new phones

Backups are encrypted files. You choose where to save them: iCloud Drive, Google Drive, your Files app or anywhere else you like. Reports (PDF, CSV and Excel) are created on your phone and go wherever you send them. We never receive your backups or reports.

On Android, the app is excluded from Google's automatic app backup, so your trips aren't copied to Google unless you save a backup there yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete any trip in the app, or export everything first and then delete the app: that removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

What the app never does

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No servers of ours: the app never uploads your data anywhere.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code from other companies.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We don't have any to sell.

Purchases and crash reports

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, not through the app's own connection. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The stores give us sales reports, such as how many copies were sold in which country. The app checks your purchase on your phone.

Crash reports: if you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash reports that show where in the app's code a crash happened. They don't contain your trips or locations. You can turn this off in your phone's privacy settings.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to reply, and delete it if you ask. Our email is hosted by Gmail (Google).

If you choose to send a diagnostic file from the app to help fix a problem, it can contain locations from your drives. It's used only to fix that problem and deleted once the issue is resolved.

Children

The app is meant for adults who drive. It doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Your app data is only on your phone, so you're in full control of it: export it or delete it in the app at any time. For anything you've emailed us, you can ask to see it or have it deleted. This applies wherever you live, including the EU and UK.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Every past version is kept in the public change history.
