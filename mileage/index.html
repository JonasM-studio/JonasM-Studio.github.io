Mileage Logbook

An automatic mileage logbook for people who drive for work. It records your drives on its own, and when it can't, it tells you.

  • Works oniPhone (iOS 17+) and Android 10+
  • Your dataStays on your phone
  • PriceFree version, or pay once
  • Reports forNZ, AU, UK, CA, US

Why it's different

  • It shows you when it may have missed a drive. A health check on the home screen warns you when a permission is off or your phone has stopped the app in the background. The app also lists times you were probably driving but no trip was recorded, so you can add them with one tap.
  • Your data stays on your phone. No account and no server. Backups are encrypted files you save wherever you like.
  • Pay once. No subscription. The free version lets you export all of your data at any time.
  • Reports with the details tax offices ask for, as PDF, CSV and Excel, including a New Zealand export laid out like the Inland Revenue logbook template.

The app shows the published mileage rates with a link to the official source. It doesn't give tax advice: check the rules that apply to you with your tax office or accountant.

Availability

Mileage Logbook is being tested on real drives before release on the App Store and Google Play. To help test the Android version, email jonasm.studio@gmail.com.
