Quillway

An automatic mileage logbook for people who drive for work. It records drives in the background and lets you know when a drive may have been missed.

  • Works oniPhone (iOS 17+) and Android 10+
  • Your dataStored on your device
  • PriceFree, with a one-time Pro purchase
  • Reports forNZ, AU, UK, CA, US

Key features

  • Missed-drive alerts. A health check flags settings that can stop recording, and the app lists times you were likely driving with no trip recorded, so you can add them.
  • Data stored on your device. No account is required. Backups are files you save to a location of your choice, with an optional password.
  • One-time purchase. Pro is not a subscription. The free version includes data export.
  • Logbook exports as PDF, CSV and Excel, including a New Zealand export based on the Inland Revenue logbook template.

Published mileage rates are shown with a link to the official source. This is not tax advice; please confirm the rules that apply to you with your tax office or accountant.

Availability

Quillway is currently in testing ahead of release on the App Store and Google Play. To take part in Android testing, email yujona.studio@gmail.com.
