Mileage Logbook support

Last updated 2 November 2026

Most missed drives happen because the phone stopped the app in the background. The fix is usually one or two settings, listed below for each phone maker.

Start with the health check.

Open the app. The health check at the top of the home screen turns red when something will stop drives from being recorded, and tells you how to fix it. When every item is green, the app is set up correctly.

Android: phone maker battery settings

Many Android phones have their own battery manager on top of Android's, and it can stop apps running in the background, sometimes for hours. Android doesn't let apps read these settings, so you need to set them yourself, once. Pick your phone:

  • OnePlus / OPPO / realme
  • Samsung
  • Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO
  • vivo / iQOO
  • Huawei / Honor
  • Google Pixel
  • Other phones

OnePlus, OPPO and realme (ColorOS / OxygenOS)

  1. Settings › Apps › App management › Mileage Logbook › Battery usage: turn on Allow background activity (and Allow foreground activity if you see it).
  2. On the same screen, turn on Auto launch. If you see Allow launch by other apps, turn it on too.
  3. Open Recent apps and lock the Mileage Logbook card: tap ⋮ above the card (or pull the card down) › Lock. A small lock appears on the card.
Check it worked: in the app's health check, switch on Done under "Phone maker background settings". If the phone stops the app again later, that item turns red and you'll get a notification.

Tested on a OnePlus 13 with Android 16. Menu names can differ slightly between versions.

Samsung (One UI)

  1. Settings › Battery › Background usage limits › Never auto sleeping apps: add Mileage Logbook.
  2. Make sure it isn't listed under Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps.
  3. Settings › Apps › Mileage Logbook › Battery: Unrestricted.
Check it worked: switch on Done under "Phone maker background settings" in the health check. If the phone stops the app again, it turns red.

Steps based on dontkillmyapp.com. If your screens look different, please email a screenshot and this page will be updated.

Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO (HyperOS / MIUI)

  1. Settings › Apps › Mileage Logbook › Autostart: on.
  2. On the same screen › Battery saver: No restrictions.
  3. Open Recent apps and lock the Mileage Logbook card (long-press it, or pull it down, and tap the lock).
Check it worked: switch on Done under "Phone maker background settings" in the health check. If the phone stops the app again, it turns red.

Steps based on dontkillmyapp.com. If your screens look different, please email a screenshot and this page will be updated.

vivo and iQOO

  1. Settings › Battery › Background power consumption management › Mileage Logbook: Allow.
  2. i Manager › App manager › Autostart manager: Mileage Logbook on.
  3. Open Recent apps and lock the Mileage Logbook card.
Check it worked: switch on Done under "Phone maker background settings" in the health check. If the phone stops the app again, it turns red.

Steps based on dontkillmyapp.com. If your screens look different, please email a screenshot and this page will be updated.

Huawei and Honor

  1. Settings › Battery › App launch › Mileage Logbook: choose Manage manually and turn on Auto-launch, Secondary launch and Run in background.
  2. Open Recent apps and pull the Mileage Logbook card down to lock it.
Check it worked: switch on Done under "Phone maker background settings" in the health check. If the phone stops the app again, it turns red.

Steps based on dontkillmyapp.com. If your screens look different, please email a screenshot and this page will be updated.

Google Pixel

Pixels don't have an extra battery manager. Settings › Apps › Mileage Logbook › App battery usage: Unrestricted is all you need.

Check it worked: every item in the app's health check is green.

Other phones

  1. Settings › Apps › Mileage Logbook › Battery: Unrestricted, or allow background activity.
  2. Allow auto-start or auto launch if your phone has that option.
  3. Lock the app in Recent apps if your phone lets you.
Check it worked: if your phone stops the app, the health check shows "Phone maker background settings" in red. Follow the steps there, then switch on Done.

Your phone maker may be listed on dontkillmyapp.com.

iPhone settings

iPhones don't have phone maker battery managers, so the standard settings are enough. In Settings › Mileage Logbook:

  • Location: Always, with Precise Location on.
  • Motion & Fitness: on.
  • Background App Refresh: on.

Low Power Mode can delay recording. The health check tells you when it's on.

Common questions

A drive wasn't recorded. What can I do?

Open the app and look at the health check first. Then look at Possibly missed: it lists times when your phone thought you were in a vehicle but no trip was recorded, and you can add the drive from there with one tap. You can also add a trip by hand.

The app says "Your phone stopped Mileage Logbook for … hours". What does that mean?

Your phone's battery manager shut the app down, and nothing could be recorded until it started again. Drives in that time may be missing. Go through the settings for your phone above. If it keeps happening after that, please get in touch.

It recorded a drive I wasn't driving

Phones can't always tell a driver from a passenger, or a car from a bus. Mark the trip as Not my drive or delete it. It won't count in your reports.

Do I need to keep the app open?

No. Once it's set up you don't need to open it at all. Please don't use Force stop: Android then won't let the app start again until you open it. On some phones, clearing all apps from Recent apps also stops it, which is why locking the card helps.

Does it use a lot of battery?

When you aren't driving, the app sleeps and waits for your phone to report movement. It only uses GPS while you're driving and for a short time after you park.

How do I move to a new phone?

Make a backup in the app and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, install the app and restore that backup from the app's backup settings.

Which mileage rate should I use?

The app includes the published rates and links to the official page for each one, but it doesn't give tax advice. Check the rules that apply to you:

  • New Zealand: Inland Revenue – Claiming vehicle expenses
  • Australia: ATO – Cents per kilometre method
  • United Kingdom: GOV.UK – Business travel and mileage
  • United States: IRS – Standard mileage rates
  • Canada: CRA – Motor vehicle expenses
